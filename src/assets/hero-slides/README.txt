TOP-OF-PAGE SLIDESHOW IMAGES
============================
Drop the pictures for the moving slideshow here (jpg / jpeg / png / webp).
They'll auto-load into the slideshow at the very top of the Home and
Tribe pages, in filename order. Prefix with numbers to control order:
  01-....jpg, 02-....jpg, 03-....jpg

Landscape/wide images work best for a full-width top banner.
